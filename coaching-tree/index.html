WHY IT MATTERS

DEVELOP PLAYERS. DEVELOP COACHES.

The coaching tree is another measure of a program's influence. Sacramento State's official list connects Christiansen to people now working in Major League scouting and field coordination, Division I head-coaching jobs, college player development and high school baseball. That network gives current Hornets exposure to a broad baseball ecosystem long after their playing careers end.
